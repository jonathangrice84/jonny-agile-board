# Business Pipeline — Agile Board (Approval-based)

This is our **single source of truth** for business execution.

**Rule:** I don’t do external actions (posting, emailing, purchasing, outreach) without a clear **Jonny Approved ✅** on the card.

## Status Columns
- **Inbox** → raw ideas/tasks
- **Backlog (Shaped)** → clarified outcome + acceptance criteria
- **Ready (Needs Approval)** → I propose actions; you approve
- **In Progress** → I’m executing (internal work allowed)
- **Blocked** → waiting on info/approval/access
- **Done** → shipped + notes

## Operating Cadence
- **Daily:** move 1 card forward.
- **Weekly:** pick 1 main bet; kill 1 weak idea.

## Board Conventions
- Titles start with a verb ("Validate…", "Draft…", "Ship…").
- One card = one outcome (not a bucket of tasks).
- If a card needs outreach/spend/public posting → it **must** sit in **Ready (Needs Approval)** until approved.
